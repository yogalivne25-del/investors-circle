How We Evaluate a Deal

Before any investment, we examine four core questions:

✓

Is there a gap between the property's price and its potential value?

✓

Is there a realistic path to enhancement or change of use?

✓

Does the risk-to-reward ratio justify the investment?

✓

Does the deal fit the strategy defined for this client?
