The Team

The people behind The Investor's Circle.

Founder & Managing Partner

Doron Afriat

Doron Afriat is a real estate entrepreneur with over 15 years of experience identifying opportunities, enhancing properties, and advising investors in Tel Aviv. His expertise is concentrated in the Old North and city center — areas where local familiarity, planning knowledge, and execution experience make the difference between an ordinary asset and a high-potential deal.

Over the years, Doron has accompanied numerous transactions and has developed deep knowledge of the local market: deal pricing, demand trends, enhancement potential, and the unique planning and legal aspects of each area.

His background combines economics and business administration, deal financing, real estate taxation, project management, and execution. Every deal is examined through three lenses: economic, planning, and commercial.

Doron's approach is grounded in personal accompaniment, transparency, and data-driven decision-making — from initial profiling, through property identification and due diligence, to purchase, enhancement, and exit. The goal remains consistent: identify the potential, manage the risk, and create real value in every transaction.

Partner, Marketing & Business Development

Yuval Lev

Yuval Lev brings over a decade of experience in marketing and business development management across companies in Israel and abroad, alongside hands-on activity in the real estate world.

His area of expertise is maximizing a property's potential after purchase — with a focus on short-term and long-term rental strategies, correct positioning, pricing, and market preparation. Yuval evaluates every property not only by its purchase price, but also by its ability to generate ongoing income, sustain demand, and support the overall investment strategy.

His goal is to accompany every client from the initial decision to full realization: from defining objectives, through selecting the rental strategy and operating the property, to sale or continued holding. Throughout the journey, the emphasis is not only on maximizing yield and economic potential, but also on developing the client as a more independent, confident, and precise investor.

Our Story

Born from the world of real estate entrepreneurship, not brokerage.

The Investor's Circle was founded from within the investment world — not the brokerage world. Over the years we have operated as investors, entrepreneurs, and project managers, accumulating hands-on experience in identifying opportunities, enhancing properties, and managing deals in the Tel Aviv market.

From that experience came the idea to give private individuals access to the same working methodology we use ourselves.
